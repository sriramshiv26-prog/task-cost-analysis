## How to Use This Template

1. **Copy this template** to a new file each week: `task-log-2026-05-18.md`
2. **After each task**, fill in the details
3. **At end of week**, fill in the weekly summary
4. **Track patterns** — what works, what doesn't
5. **Use findings** to optimize next week's model selection

---

## Metrics to Track Over Time

- **Success rate by model** — which models deliver best results
- **Time savings** — track hours saved vs manual work
- **Cost per task** — track to stay under $120/month budget
- **Patterns** — which task types work best with which models
- **Escalations** — when local models get stuck
- **ROI** — cost savings from automation + time savings

---

## Integration with Monthly Report

At month end, aggregate weekly logs into `monthly-cost-report.md`:
1. Total tasks completed
2. Total cost ($0 vs $ spent)
3. Success rates
4. Patterns observed
5. Recommendations for next month

---

## Tips for Consistent Logging

- **Keep it brief** — 30 seconds per entry
- **Be honest** — log failures too (they're learning opportunities)
- **Note patterns** — "qwen-1.5b always fails on complex logic" is valuable
- **Update in real-time** — don't wait until end of week
- **Review weekly** — use it to adjust strategy
